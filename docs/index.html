Our Story

Built for real
drop-in play

Serve Sports is a drop-in badminton community in Snoqualmie, WA. Every Monday and Thursday evening, 30–50 players show up to play doubles on two courts — no pre-registration, no fixed teams, just show up and hit.

For years, a physical whiteboard tracked who was here and how everyone was doing. But math errors, illegible names, and arguments over the tally were a recurring theme.

Snobaddy was built to replace that whiteboard — and do everything it couldn't. Season-long stats, AI match suggestions, finals tournaments, player profiles, and more. All from the phone in your pocket.

Three Ways to Score

However your night runs,
we've got a mode for it

Different sessions run differently. Snobaddy adapts to your night — not the other way around.

⚔️ Live Match Mode

Record Every Match

The full live experience. As each match finishes, an admin picks the 4 players and enters the score. The scoreboard updates instantly for everyone.

  • Pick 4 checked-in players per match
  • Enter final score — winner determined automatically
  • Live scoreboard visible to all players
  • Full match history saved at session close
📋 Whiteboard Mode

Tally at End of Night

Already tracking W/L on a physical board? Just enter the final tally when the session closes. Great for nights when someone else is keeping track.

  • Enter total wins and losses per player
  • Built-in balance check (W = L across all players)
  • Inline edit stepper to adjust scores in-place
  • Feeds into the same season leaderboard
📷 AI Photo Import

Snap the Board

Already have a whiteboard full of names and tallies? Take a photo and let Claude AI read it. Names and scores are filled in automatically.

  • Upload a photo of the physical whiteboard
  • AI extracts names and W/L counts
  • Unmatched names flagged for manual resolution
  • Review, adjust, and save in seconds

Season Finals

End the season
with a proper tournament

Go beyond regular sessions. Run a full end-of-season finals with group stages, standings, and knockout rounds — all inside the app.

🏟️

Multiple Groups

Split players into Groups A, B, C, S — any number of pools. Each group runs its own round-robin stage.

🔄

Round Robin Format

Every pair plays every other pair in the group. Standings update automatically as results are entered.

⚡

Playoff Advancement

Configure how many advance — Top 2, Top 4 — and who they face in the knockout stage.

🏅

Gold, Silver & Bronze

Separate brackets ensure every team plays a final match — gold/silver final and a 3rd place play-off.

🤝

Pair Configuration

Manually set up doubles pairs for the finals or carry over season partners — full admin control.

📋

Separate Session

Finals run as their own session type — they don't pollute regular season stats or leaderboard data.

How It Works

From the front door
to the final score

1

Admin opens the session

One tap before play starts. The session goes live and players can start checking in.

2

Players check in

Everyone opens the app and taps "I'm here." No clipboard, no shouting. Admins can check in players too.

3

Record matches live

After each game, pick the 4 players and enter the score. Or use the AI match suggester to decide who plays next.

4

Close & celebrate

Admin closes the session. Session awards are announced, tonight's stats are locked in, and the leaderboard updates.
