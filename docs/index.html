What it does

Everything the whiteboard did —
and a whole lot more

Built for the reality of drop-in play: no pre-registration, no fixed teams, just show up and play.

✅

Check In From Your Phone

Players tap once to mark themselves present. Admins can check in or out anyone in the room.

⚔️

Record Match Results

Pick 4 players, enter the score. Win/loss totals update instantly on the live scoreboard.

🏆

Season Leaderboard

Win %, total matches, and rankings across every session — all season long. Bragging rights included.

🤖

AI Match Suggestions

Let the app propose balanced doubles pairings based on skill levels. Fair fights, better games.

📷

Photo Score Import

Snap a photo of the whiteboard tally — Claude AI reads the names and scores and fills the form for you.

🏅

Session Awards

The Slayer, The Untouchable, The Point Collector — end-of-night highlights for the night's best performances.

How It Works

From front door
to final score

1

Admin opens the session

One tap before play starts. The session is live — players can now check in.

2

Players check themselves in

Everyone opens the app and taps "I'm here." No paper list, no shouting across the room.

3

Record each match

Pick 4 checked-in players, enter the score. The scoreboard updates live for everyone.

4

Close & celebrate

Admin closes the session. Session awards are revealed — season stats updated automatically.
