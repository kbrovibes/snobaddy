# v0.17.14 — Award ties: show all tied players

## What changed
Session award cards now show all players who share the top stat, sorted alphabetically and joined with commas.

Previously only one player was shown (alphabetical tiebreak silently dropped the rest).

## Files modified
| File | Change |
|---|---|
| `src/lib/db/matches.ts` | `SessionHighlights` interface: `name: string` → `names: string[]` for sultan, ironShuttle, untouchable, cannon. Computation now collects all tied players at the max value. |
| `src/components/SessionHighlights.tsx` | Renders `names.map(getFirstName).join(", ")` instead of `getFirstName(name)`. |

## Notes
- noMercy (team award) is unchanged — it remains a single winning team pair.
- Ties are compared after rounding for untouchable (win pct rounded to nearest integer %).
