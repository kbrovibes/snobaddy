# v0.39.2 — Newsletter Mode Breakdown + Attendance Fix

Date: 2026-05-15

## Two related issues

### 1. Intro made the numbers look wrong even when they were right
The v0.39.1 newsletter said "15 regular sessions, 127 scored matches, 1611 individual battles". Mathematically valid, but the three numbers come from two different tracking modes and the reader has no way to know that:
- `127` matches and the 5.4-point average margin are only from the **full-detail** subset of nights, where every match was logged with team rosters and scores.
- `1611` outcomes is the sum across **all** nights, including whiteboard-tally nights that don't have per-match data at all.

Rewriting the intro so the two modes are called out separately removes the apparent inconsistency.

### 2. `sessions_attended` undercounted whiteboard nights
The previous implementation pulled `session_attended` only from `session_players` rows. On whiteboard-mode nights, players often don't formally check in — they just appear in the tally photo and get a `session_tally` row. Those nights were dropping out of the attendance count, which then poisoned downstream metrics like "wins per session" and "perfect attendance".

Now a player counts as having been at a session if any of the following is true:
- `session_players` row exists for them at that session
- `session_tally` row exists for them at that session with `wins + losses > 0`
- They appear in any match roster at that session

## Snapshot surface

`SeasonStatsSnapshot` gains three new fields, all populated by `getSeasonStats`:

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `full_detail_session_count` | Sessions where individual matches were logged with scores |
| `tally_session_count` | Sessions tracked only via whiteboard tally |
| `total_player_outcomes` | Sum of `wins + losses` across every player (both modes combined) |

`real_session_count` continues to be the union of the two — used for the headline number in the intro and as the perfect-attendance denominator.

For Spring 2026 the new snapshot resolves to: 15 nights total, 5 full-detail + 10 tally-only, 127 scored matches with average margin 5.4 (close: 20, blowouts: 4, bagels: 0), 1,611 total player outcomes across 41 unique humans. Re-seeded as v3 in `season_newsletters`.
