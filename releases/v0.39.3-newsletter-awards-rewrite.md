# v0.39.3 — Newsletter Awards Rewrite + Warmer Intro

Date: 2026-05-15

## What changed

### 1. Intro is no longer a fact dump
The previous opener led with "1,611 individual W/L outcomes logged across the two Snoqualmie courts" — accurate, useless. Reads now in a narrator voice that frames the season ("knees protested, the kids laughed at the grown-ups taking themselves too seriously") and points the reader at the section structure. No bare counts in the first 200 words.

### 2. Awards now mirror the leaderboard
The old `🏆 The Nutcracker Awards` block (eight one-line gags — best win, coldest stat line, best/worst duo, telepathy, surge, humility) is gone. In its place are the two awards that the leaderboard itself surfaces, computed with the same logic:

- **🥜 Badminton Nut** — top 3 by games played, tiebreak by name asc.
- **✂️ Nut Cracker** — top 3 by win %, filtered to players with at least `floor(top_nut_games / 2)` games and `> 0` games. Same minimum threshold the leaderboard uses.

Winner gets a `🏆` line with a sentence of color; both runners-up get `🥈` bullet lines with their actual numbers. Eligibility is computed first, then award math, then prose — so the runners-up always line up with what `LeaderboardTable.tsx` would display.

### 3. Self-recusal mechanism
Sometimes a regular doesn't want to be in the awards. Generator now reads `AWARD_EXCLUDE_NAMES` (lowercased name set, hardcoded in `src/lib/newsletter/generate.ts`) and drops those players from the award eligibility lists before sorting.

First entry: **Sekhar Durga**, who has politely declined. He still appears in match data, the math corner, UBR sections, and pair stats. The newsletter ends with a one-line footnote acknowledging his record (currently 64-40 across 104 games at 61.5%) so readers know the bar without the awards naming him.

To opt someone else in or out later: edit the `AWARD_EXCLUDE_NAMES` constant. If we ever need this to be data-driven, the right place is a column on `players` (e.g. `excluded_from_awards bool`).

### 4. Fun Observations expanded to ~17 bullets
The old list had ~6. The new list adds a mix of:
- **Tracking-mode mix** for the season
- **Close-match percentage** and **average margin** (scoped to scored matches)
- **Most-played frequent partnership**
- **Strongest vs unluckiest** frequent duo (compared in percentage points)
- **Clean-sweep partnerships** (4+ games, 0 losses) and **winless partnerships** (3+ games, 0 wins)
- **Underdog crew** (level ≤ 2 with ≥ 55% win rate)
- **Slumpers** (level ≥ 4 with ≤ 50% win rate)
- **Wins-per-session champion**
- **Biggest UBR shake-up** in absolute terms
- **Top-3 UBR risers** as a cohort
- **Top-2 UBR sliders** as a cohort
- **Tally-mode workload** for the recorder
- **Gender split** of court time + top women's win rate
- **Best cross-skill partnership** (mixed skill levels)
- **Drop-in count** (players who played only a handful of games)
- **Roster breadth** (total unique humans)
- **Sekhar footnote**

Some bullets only appear when the data supports them (e.g. undefeated runs, mixed-skill pairs) — the section grows or shrinks gracefully without producing empty placeholders.
