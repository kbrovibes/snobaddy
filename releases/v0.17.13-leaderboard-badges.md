# v0.17.13 — Leaderboard badge refresh

## What changed
- Rank #1 (by win%) badge changed from 🥇 to 🏆
- Silver (🥈) and bronze (🥉) badges removed; those rows now show plain numbers
- Only the gold row retains its yellow background highlight
- Player with the highest `matches_played` earns a 🥜 badge (shown instead of their number rank)
- If the top win% player also has the most matches, 🏆 takes precedence
