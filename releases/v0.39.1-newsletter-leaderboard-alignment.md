# v0.39.1 — Newsletter Stats Aligned With Leaderboard + Godmode Regenerate

Date: 2026-05-15

## Bug

The v0.39.0 newsletter generator was undercounting players because it took `max(match_wins, tally_wins)` and `max(match_losses, tally_losses)` per player. The leaderboard sums those two sources. For Spring 2026 the gap was big — e.g. Miz Rahman read as 44 wins / 39 losses (83 games) in the old newsletter but is 65 wins / 55 losses (120 games) on the leaderboard.

## Fix

`src/lib/newsletter/stats.ts` now mirrors `getActivePlayers` in `src/lib/db/players.ts`:

- Per-player **wins = sum(match wins) + sum(tally wins)** (same for losses).
- Eligible sessions are non-test sessions on or before `stats_lock_date`. The previous `status = 'completed'` and `session_type = 'regular'` filters were removed — match_type and absence of tally rows on non-played sessions already exclude what we need to exclude.
- Matches involving any soft-deleted player are skipped (same exclusion as leaderboard).
- Roster output is filtered to `onboarding_complete = true` + non-deleted players.
- Perfect-attendance denominator is "sessions in scope that actually produced results" (had at least one match or tally row).

## Godmode regenerate

- `POST /api/admin/seasons/[id]/newsletter` now requires `auth.isGodMode`. Plain admins get a 403.
- `/admin/seasons/[id]/newsletter` page still renders for any admin, but the regenerate form only appears for god-mode users. Other admins see a note pointing them at god-mode users.
